A lightweight multi-channel click to chat plugin. Add a floating social chat widget and versatile redirect buttons for instant communication.

== Description ==

**Click to Chat** is a high-performance **multi-channel click to chat WordPress plugin** designed to bridge the gap between you and your visitors. Instead of a single-link tool, this plugin provides an all-in-one **floating chat widget** that supports multiple messaging and social platforms in one unified, sleek interface.

The plugin features a stylish, expandable **floating button** that acts as a central communication hub. Whether you need a **social messenger redirect**, a **floating contact button**, or a direct link to your professional profiles, this plugin handles various channels simultaneously with zero impact on site performance.

### Why Use a Click to Chat Plugin?
* Add a floating WhatsApp chat button to WordPress
* Allow visitors to contact you instantly from any page
* Offer multiple communication options in a single chat widget
* Improve lead generation and customer support accessibility
* Maintain fast page speed and privacy compliance

### 🚀 All-in-One Multi-Channel Widget
In 2026, accessibility is key. This **universal social chat widget** allows you to offer your customers their preferred choice of communication, reducing friction and increasing lead generation. It is the perfect **floating chat for WooCommerce**, business portfolios, and service-based websites that need to capture inquiries across different social ecosystems.

### 💬 Versatile Social Redirect Features
Why limit your site to just one app? Our **floating button** supports a wide range of **instant redirect** options:
* **Social Messaging:** Direct links to the world's most popular messaging and chat apps.
* **Instant Redirects:** Seamlessly transition users from your site to your social chat inboxes.
* **Direct Voice & Text:** Integrated "Click to Call" and SMS functionality for mobile users.
* **Professional Networks:** Quick-access links for business and networking platforms.

### 🛡️ Privacy & Performance First
Click to Chat is a **privacy-first social chat widget**. It is fully GDPR-ready because it does **not** track users, set cookies, or load heavy third-party scripts. It is a lightweight solution optimized for **Core Web Vitals**, ensuring your **floating contact button** never slows down your page speed.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/dragwyb-click-to-chat` directory, or install it through the WordPress Plugins screen.
2. Activate the plugin through the **Plugins** menu.
3. Open **Click to Chat** from the WordPress admin menu.
4. Enable your preferred chat channels and customize the widget.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What is a click to chat widget? =
A click to chat widget is a floating button that lets website visitors instantly contact you via various social messaging apps, email, or phone.

= Does this plugin support multiple chat platforms? =
Yes. Dragwyb Click to Chat is a multi-channel tool that allows you to add several different social and contact redirect buttons in one widget.

= Does this plugin store or track user data? =
No. The plugin does not store cookies, track users, or collect analytics. It only creates outbound links to third-party communication platforms.

= Is this plugin suitable for business websites and eCommerce? =
Yes. It is ideal for businesses, online stores, agencies, freelancers, and service providers looking for an omnichannel contact solution.

= Will this plugin slow down my website? =
No. The plugin is lightweight, optimized for performance, and loads minimal assets to ensure fast page speeds.
