== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress Admin, go to **Plugins → Add New**.
2. Search for **Dragwyb Click to Chat**.
3. Click **Install Now**, then click **Activate**.
4. Click on **Click to Chat** in your WordPress admin menu.
5. Follow the quick **Setup Wizard** to configure your AI keys, knowledge base, support desk, and contact channels.
6. (Optional) Paste `[dctc_support_portal]` on any page to display your Customer Support Desk.

== Shortcodes Reference ==

* `[dctc_support_portal]` – Displays the full interactive customer support desk (Submit Ticket, My Tickets, and Guest Access tabs).

* `[dctc_support_ticket_form]` – Displays a standalone ticket submission form. Supports attributes:
  * `inline="true"` – Embeds form directly inline.
  * `trigger_id="my-custom-btn"` – Opens ticket modal when clicking an existing button with that HTML ID.
  * *(Alias: `[dctc_support_ticket_form]`)*

* `[dctc_support_user_tickets]` – Displays a filterable customer ticket history table with status badges and thread access.
  * *(Alias: `[dctc_support_user_tickets]`)*

* `[dctc-widget]` – Embeds the omnichannel floating contact widget on specific pages or templates.

* `[dctc_ai]` – Embeds the AI Chatbot assistant window or launcher button.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is this plugin really free? =
Yes! The Free version provides a complete AI chatbot, RAG knowledge base (SQLite & Pinecone), customer support ticketing helpdesk, WooCommerce sales assistant, lead capture engine, and multi-channel floating contact button.

= Do I need my own AI API key (BYOK)? =
Yes. You connect your own OpenAI (ChatGPT) or Google Gemini API key. You pay the AI provider directly based on your usage, without any subscription markups or middleman fees.

= How does the chatbot know about my website? =
The plugin indexes your WordPress pages, posts, documentation, and WooCommerce products into vector embeddings. When a visitor asks a question, the chatbot retrieves relevant excerpts from your site and generates an accurate answer with verified clickable source links.

= Can I use this on websites without WooCommerce? =
Yes! The plugin works on any WordPress site (blogs, agencies, portfolios, service businesses). When WooCommerce is installed and active, product recommendations, product cards, 1-click cart, and order tracking activate automatically.

= How do guest visitors submit and track support tickets? =
Guest visitors can submit tickets through the support portal. They receive an email confirmation verification alert before submission to prevent typos, and a secure tokenized tracking link via email. An on-screen session countdown timer protects privacy. If a guest later registers or logs in with that same email address, their past guest tickets automatically link to their WordPress account.

= Can I disable features I don't use to optimize my site speed? =
Yes! Navigate to **Click to Chat → Settings → General**. You can independently enable or disable any module (Channels Widget, AI Chatbot Assistant, or Customer Support Center). When a module is turned off, its JavaScript, CSS, and backend queries are completely stopped from loading, ensuring zero bloat and maximum performance.

= What does Click to Chat Pro include? =
Click to Chat Pro adds Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Groq, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, and local Ollama models, Automatic Provider Failover, 1-Click AI Reply Suggestions for agents, AI Ticket Summaries, Auto Ticket Assignment, CRM Webhooks (Zapier/Make), Admin AI Copilot, and Advanced Usage Limits.

== External Services ==

This plugin connects to external third-party services only when configured and enabled by an administrator:

* **OpenAI API:** When enabled, user prompts, chat messages, and knowledge base text are sent to OpenAI to generate responses and search embeddings.
  * [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use)
  * [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
* **Google Gemini API:** When enabled, chat inquiries and knowledge base text are sent to Google AI to generate responses and embeddings.
  * [Google Gemini API Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)
  * [Google AI Privacy Policy](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/usage-policies)
* **Pinecone:** When selected as your vector storage provider, text embeddings are stored in Pinecone indexes for semantic search.
  * [Pinecone Terms of Service](https://www.pinecone.io/terms/)
  * [Pinecone Privacy Policy](https://www.pinecone.io/privacy/)
* **Dragwyb Feedback API:** Optional diagnostic or deactivation feedback is sent to https://feedback.dragwyb.com only when explicitly submitted by an administrator.
